# Phased build prompts

1. **Evidence ledger:** “Add authenticated organizations, Postgres migrations and an immutable evidence record for market signals. Preserve the current static demo. Test tenant isolation, stale evidence and rollback.”
2. **Rights gate:** “Add a territory/language rights checklist and signed-document metadata. Block production status until required grants are approved. Do not store contract files in public storage.”
3. **Demand experiments:** “Create localized landing-page experiments with consented analytics and refundable deposits. Name payment, timeout, duplicate-click and refund failures.”
4. **Localization QA:** “Add transcript/slide ingestion, bilingual glossary, human approval and quiz-parity evaluation. AI output remains draft until approved.”
5. **Pilot operations:** “Add production milestones, seller attribution, learner support, refunds and margin reconciliation. Instrument conversion, completion, critical defects and contribution.”
6. **Portfolio learning:** “Only after 10 pilots, train/calibrate ranking against outcomes and publish confidence intervals, evidence age and score explanations.”
